Sally | Staff dashboard

All chats

Switch Sally off to stop replies everywhere, and back on when your team has finished handling queries. Scheduled reminders are not affected.

Import existing chats

Paste one chat per line as number, name (a CSV works too). Numbers starting with + or 00 are used as written, 8-digit numbers are treated as Singapore (+65), and numbers starting with 0 as Indonesian (+62). Pick OFF to keep Sally silent for these chats before they next message.

Individual chats

Chats appear here after their first message. When a supported WhatsApp Business app message echo is received, Sally is switched off for that chat until staff turn her back on. Chats with Sally switched off are listed first.
